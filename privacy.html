Privacy Policy

Information about how the Planets Z mobile application handles information and third-party services.

Last updated: October 4, 2026
01

Information We Collect

Planets Z does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.

Planets Z is designed to provide its functionality without requiring users to provide personal account information.

02

Third-Party Services

Planets Z uses NASA APIs to retrieve astronomy and Earth observation information and images.

The application may also use third-party services required to provide application functionality. These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

03

Internet Connection

Planets Z requires an internet connection to retrieve content from third-party APIs and display remote images.

04

Data Storage

Planets Z does not operate its own user database and does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

05

Children's Privacy

Planets Z does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

06

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

07

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the application's store listing.
